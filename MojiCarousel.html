Privacy Policy

Moji Carousel (package com.endless.mojicarousel) · Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Bahasa Indonesia di bawah

This policy explains what information the game "Moji Carousel" (the "Game"), published by HenHimA1 ("we", "us"), handles. The Game collects very little.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of our own. We do not receive your personal information.
  • Your progress (level, gold, lives, boosters and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising data as described below.
  • Optional in-app purchases (gold packs) are handled entirely by Google Play.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings in a local file on your device. This file is never sent to us. Android may back it up to your Google account through Android Auto Backup if you have device backup turned on; that copy is managed by Google. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes the local copy.

The Game does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to get a life or free gold). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its ad partners may collect and use, for example:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in Android settings),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • information about ad interactions and diagnostics.

This data is used to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where permitted, to personalise ads. We do not receive this data. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where required, the Game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can choose whether ads are personalised. You can play the Game fully without watching any ad. You can change personalised-ad settings in Android under Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (gold packs). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the Game only that a purchase was completed.

5. Analytics and crash reports

The Game does not include its own analytics or tracking tools. Google Play may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (installs, crashes) through the Play Console.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through ads or otherwise, contact us and we will help.

7. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies, linked above. Progress data stays on your device (and in your Android backup, if enabled) until you delete it.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all Game data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.
  • Limit ads personalisation: Android Settings → Google → Ads, or reset/delete your advertising ID.
  • Skip ads: ads are always optional.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
